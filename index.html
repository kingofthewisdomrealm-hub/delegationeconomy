The Delegation Economy

AI agents are becoming the buyer. People will not stop using the internet. They will stop personally operating it.

The click disappears.

Your funnel assumes a human is somewhere in the loop — browsing, comparing, hesitating, clicking. The new buyer never arrives at the page.

Lazy loyalty disappears.

Nine steps to cancel, and most people give up at four. An agent gets to step nine before you finish breakfast.

Manipulation becomes evidence.

Fake urgency works on people who are tired. An agent is not tired, and it remembers what your countdown clock said yesterday.
