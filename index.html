The case against

This isn't guaranteed, and anyone who tells you it is should worry you.

Here's what would make the whole thesis fail:

  • Agents stay unreliable enough that people keep doing the buying themselves.
  • Consumers refuse to hand over payment authority, and trust never arrives.
  • Regulators restrict what an agent may transact on a person’s behalf.
  • Platforms lock agents out to protect ad revenue, and the open web wins by default.

Any one of those slows this down by years. None is a reason to be unreadable to a machine in the meantime — that part costs almost nothing and helps either way.
