Delegation Economy 01 / 06

You want more done. Without adding more people.

That is the whole story. Every company in this market is chasing the same thing.

Your software still waits on you.

You click. It moves. You stop. It stops. You are the bottleneck, and you can feel it.

What it looks like

A pile of tools. A pile of tabs. Smart people copying, pasting, and checking the same work, all day.

What it costs

Time. Money. Energy. And a quiet fear that the next company will figure this out first.

Agents don’t wait. You name the goal. They go get it.

Old way

You decide every step. Software does one task. You check it. Then you start the next step.

New way

You say what winning looks like. The agent plans, acts, fixes mistakes, and comes back with the result.

Four things to see clearly

1. Doing the work gets cheap

Jobs that needed a full team will cost almost nothing once an agent can finish them on its own.

2. Your job is to aim

The hard part is no longer the work. The hard part is picking the goal and keeping the agent honest.

3. Buy results, not buttons

People will stop paying for software that sits there. They will pay for work that gets done.

4. Teams of agents win

The companies that treat agents like staff will pass the ones that treat them like a nicer search box.

10–100×

That is what it feels like when the work keeps moving after you leave the room.

If you wait

You keep hiring for tasks an agent could finish. You stay busy. You fall behind.

If you move

You set better goals. You ship more. You spend people on judgment, not busywork.

Pick one goal. Hand it to an agent. See what comes back.

The market is not asking “what can AI do?” It is asking what you are willing to stop doing yourself.

One email. No cadence. Unsubscribe in one click.
